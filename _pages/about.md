I am a PhD Student in Hugh Safford's Vegetation and Fire Ecology Lab at UC Davis in the Gradaute Group in Ecology. 

I am an avian ecologist and I ask questions about birds to understand the impact of changing fire and disturbance regimes on terrestrial ecosystems. My goal is to produce research that aligns land manager objectives and bird conservation goals. Additionally, I strive to conduct research that informs conservation and management actions for birds and their ecosystems in California and foster collaborations with community partners for multiple benefits conservation.
